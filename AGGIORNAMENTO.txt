PilotLog 12.3 — Payroll Regime MAC / IBC

- Settings > Payroll Regime: IBC or MAC.
- IBC module and formulas remain unchanged.
- MAC adds the 2026 Air Arabia Maroc employee-tax calculation reconstructed from supplied payslips.
- MAC separates observed taxable/non-taxable components, CNSS, AMO, CIMR, Ass. Maladie, professional expense deduction, net imposable and IR.
- Regular MAC pay is tax-protected/grossed to preserve the contractual net target. Positive arrears/adjustments are taxable and not gross-protected.
- MAC family deduction count is editable in Settings.
- Payroll opens according to the regime selected in Settings.
- Recalculate stores the selected regime in the monthly Payroll extras snapshot.
- No automatic fallback between MAC and IBC.
